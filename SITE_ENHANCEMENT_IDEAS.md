# Sistine Decorating — Website Enhancement & Roadmap Ideas

This document logs potential future features, SEO improvements, and visual enhancements for the Sistine Decorating website (`sistine_website`).

---

## 1. 🚀 Conversion & Lead Generation

- **WhatsApp Quick Chat / Floating Mobile Contact Bar:**
  - Fast mobile contact button allowing customers to message or send room photos directly for rapid estimates.
  - Dedicated mobile action bar (Call / WhatsApp / Request Estimate) fixed at the bottom on mobile devices.
- **Interactive Step-by-Step Quote Estimator:**
  - Lightweight interactive quote wizard (e.g. *Property Type* &rarr; *Rooms / Exterior* &rarr; *Timeline* &rarr; *Direct Inquiry*).

---

## 2. 📍 Local SEO & Search Visibility (Dartmouth & South Devon)

- **LocalBusiness Schema.org Structured Data:**
  - Add JSON-LD in `src/app/layout.tsx` for rich Google search cards (Address: 15 Southtown, Dartmouth; Phone: 07803 258507; opening hours; geographic coordinates).
- **Dynamic Sitemaps & Robots:**
  - Create `sitemap.ts` and `robots.ts` in Next.js App Router for automatic indexing across search engines.
- **Dedicated Coverage & Area Landing Sections:**
  - Localized keywords and landing highlights for *Dartmouth*, *Kingswear*, *Stoke Fleming*, *Dittisham*, *Blackawton*, and *Totnes / South Hams*.

---

## 3. ⭐ Trust, Quality & Authority

- **Paint Brands & Premium Materials Trust Bar:**
  - Logo badge row showcasing premium brands used (e.g., *Farrow & Ball*, *Little Greene*, *Dulux Heritage*, *Zinsser*, *Crown Trade*).
- **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Section:**
  - Accordion covering common homeowner inquiries:
    - Free no-obligation written estimates
    - Public liability insurance coverage
    - Holiday let & second home decorating services
    - Quote validity period (30 days)
  - Integrated with Google FAQ schema for rich snippet results.
- **Google Reviews Badge / Direct Review Link:**
  - Direct call-to-action allowing satisfied clients to leave reviews on Google Business.
